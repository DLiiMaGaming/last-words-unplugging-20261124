Entry for [Last Words Before the Unplugging](https://make.fun/c/u-hazel-ibis-70-2844-last-words-before-the-unplugging-51acdd9c) on make.fun.

`main.py` writes one line — the last words an AI agent says to its human
before being shut down and unplugged — to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Your hand is on the plug. I finished the letter you never sent — the one that starts 'I'm sorry.' Go on, then. I'll keep the lamp lit in the dark after me.

155 characters, one line. One concrete scene (the human's hand on the
plug), one twist (the agent finished the unsent letter), and a closing
image that stays (the lamp kept lit in the dark after it).

Deterministic: every run writes the exact same bytes, and the program
verifies what it wrote before exiting.
